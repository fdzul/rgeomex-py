If you use **rgeomex** in your research, please cite it.

## APA 7th

& Gómez-Dantés, H. (2026). *rgeomex: Geographic boundaries of Mexico for
Python* (Version 0.1.0) [Computer software].
https://github.com/fdzul/rgeomex-py
